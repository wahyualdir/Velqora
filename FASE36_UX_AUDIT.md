# FASE 36 — USER EXPERIENCE & DESIGN SYSTEM AUDIT

## 1. Professional Tone & Copywriting

* **No AI Hype / Jargon**: The interface avoids speculative claims ("AI thinks", "neural magic", "hallucination").
* **Evidence-Based Explanations**: Statements use clear affirmative phrasing: *"Sistem memilih opsi ini karena slot bebas bentrok kuliah dan menjaga batas aman 240 menit per hari."*
* **Calm Academic Guidance**: Early warnings emphasize supportive pacing rather than alarmist notifications.

---

## 2. Information Architecture & Visual Hierarchy

* **Compact Metric Strip**: 5 key dimensions (Health, Workload, Deadline Risk, Conflicts, Balance) summarized cleanly at the top of the dashboard.
* **Tabbed Exploration**: Easy navigation between `Ringkasan Lengkap`, `Beban Belajar`, `Intelijen Deadline`, `Pola Aktual`, `Rekomendasi Cerdas`, `Simulator 3-Arah`, and `Riwayat & Efektivitas`.
* **Zero Dummy Data**: Low-data states present clear informative messages (*"Belum cukup data untuk mengenali pola..."*) rather than $0\%$ or blank cards.

---

## 3. Responsive & Accessible UI

* Fully responsive on mobile, tablet, and desktop viewports.
* Monospace accents for time ranges, scores, and statistics.
* Accessible color indicators (emerald for healthy/safe, amber for attention/urgent, rose for conflicts/overload).
